Questions

Before you download

Is this the official Life in the UK test app?

No. This is an independent revision tool with no affiliation to the Home Office or UK Visas and Immigration. Content is based on Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents (3rd edition), published under the Open Parliament Licence. Always use the official handbook as your primary source.

Do I need to pay to use it?

No. 85 questions are free on both web and iOS — five study modes on the web, seven on iOS, with no ads and no account. Pro unlocks all 794 questions for £2.99 lifetime or £1.99/month.

What's actually on the real test?

24 multiple-choice questions in 45 minutes. You need 18 correct — 75% — to pass. You can retake it as many times as you need.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The iOS app works fully offline, and the web version keeps working after the first load.

Do you collect any of my data?

None. No analytics, no tracking, no ad IDs, no account. Your progress lives on your device and nowhere else.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — but the free web app runs in any mobile browser, and you can add it to your home screen.
