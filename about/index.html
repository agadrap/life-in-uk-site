About

Hi, I'm Aga.

I built Life in the UK: Flashcards while preparing for my own Life in the UK test. Everything that confused me — which Henry did what, how the courts fit together, why 1707 matters — became a diagram instead of a flashcard. I passed. Then I made it free.

The rest of the time I'm a data analytics engineer and developer based in England. I make things across different worlds: data pipelines, web apps, film reviews, and a project I'm not ready to talk about yet. Curiosity is the thread that ties it all together.

You can find the rest of what I do at byaga.dev, or reach me at hello@byaga.dev.
